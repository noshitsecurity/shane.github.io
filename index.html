My name is Shane.

I am a cybersecurity architect, instructor, and the founder of NoShitSecurity.

My work is a mix of enforcing resilient design patterns that maintain a theat-informed defense, and challenging or disrupting norms to provoke industry evolution. You can view my technical certifications directly via Credly and see my professional history through my verified LinkedIn Profile.

About Me

For more than 25 years, my work has focused on hardening defenses, optimizing architecture, and engineering environments designed to withstand persistent actors and supply-chain vulnerabilities.

In September of 2020, I became the founder of a 501(c)3 nonprofit business called NoShitSecurity, where I designed the Jurassic Jungle® Information Security Internship program, establishing several enterprise partnerships to help train and certify the next generation of cybersecurity professionals.

Beyond the technology sector, my analytical approach and steadfast resilience were heavily tested through an intense personal chapter involving housing instability and navigating the legal system.

As a self-represented (pro se) litigant, I managed a rigorous legal defense from Municipal up to the Supreme Court of Texas in the matter of Shane Lilly v. The State of Texas, fighting to both protect the life and rehabilitation of my dog, Major, and to close legal loopholes in Texas Health & Safety Code.
